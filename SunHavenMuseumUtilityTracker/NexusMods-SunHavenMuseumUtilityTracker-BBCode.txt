[b][size=5]Sun Haven Museum Utility Tracker (S.M.U.T.)[/size][/b]

Track museum donations across [b]Hall of Gems[/b], [b]Hall of Culture[/b], and the [b]Aquarium[/b] in [url=https://store.steampowered.com/app/1432860/Sun_Haven/]Sun Haven[/url]. Lists use the game's real item IDs (from [b]Wish.ItemID[/b] and official bundle layouts), so icons, search, and cross-mod features stay aligned with your save.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Three halls[/b] — Gems, Culture, and Aquarium bundles in one parchment-style tracker
[*][b]Progress[/b] — Per-section and per-bundle counts; overall completion; optional in-game bundle stats next to headers
[*][b]Icons[/b] — Item icons from game data when available
[*][b]Search & filter[/b] — Find by name; toggle “needed only” to hide completed rows
[*][b]Per-character saves[/b] — Progress stored separately for each character
[*][b]Hotkeys[/b] — Default [b]Ctrl+C[/b] to open/close; [b]F7[/b] alternate toggle (no modifier, e.g. Steam Deck)
[*][b]Display[/b] — Configurable UI scale (50%–250%)
[*][b]Sync with Game[/b] — One-click import: marks every item in [b]completed[/b] bundles using world save progress
[*][b]Live donations[/b] — Donating at the museum chest can auto-mark the matching tracker row (bundle-scoped when the same item appears in multiple bundles)
[/list]

Other mods can read donation state via [b]Plugin.GetDonationManager()[/b] when integrations exist (e.g. Senpai's Chest, Sunhaven Todo, Trinket Fortune, Haven's Almanac, Haven Dev Tools).

[line]

[size=4][b]How tracking works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The vanilla game records [b]bundle-level[/b] progress, not a full per-slot checklist for incomplete bundles.
[*]S.M.U.T. keeps a [b]checklist[/b] in its own save; you can toggle rows manually.
[*][b]Sync with Game[/b] reads world progress and, for each bundle the game treats as [b]done[/b], marks [b]all[/b] items in that bundle in the tracker. It does not reconstruct partial bundles item-by-item.
[*]After major list updates, old saves may have placeholder row keys — use [b]Sync[/b] or re-check affected bundles if counts look wrong.
[/list]

[spoiler][size=3][b]Museum coverage (summary)[/b][/size]
[b]Hall of Gems:[/b] Mana, Money, Golden, Bars, Gems, Nel'Vari Mines, Withergate Mines — quantities match in-game bundle requirements where applicable.

[b]Hall of Culture:[/b] Spring/Summer/Fall/Winter crops, Nel'Vari & Withergate crops, Flowers, Foraging, Exploration, Combat, Alchemy, Nel'Vari Temple — static lists with real IDs.

[b]Aquarium:[/b] Fishing treasures, seasonal fish tanks, Nel'Vari / Withergate / large fish tanks — static fish lists.
[/spoiler]
