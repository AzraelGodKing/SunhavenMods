[b][size=5]Trinket Fortune[/size][/b]

Increases the odds of [b]unowned fishing trinkets[/b] dropping as you donate to the museum aquarium. Fish that grant aquarium trinkets benefit — less frustrating grind for the last few catches.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Museum-aware[/b] — Bonus scales with aquarium completion; unowned trinkets get better odds as you progress
[*][b]Configurable[/b] — Bonus strength, minimum progress gate, and a hard cap on bonus chance
[*][b]Standalone-safe[/b] — Loads without Haven Dev Tools; optional integrations when those mods are present
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]BepInEx/config/TrinketFortune.cfg[/b] (first run copies from legacy [b]com.azraelgodking.trinketfortune.cfg[/b] if present)

[list]
[*][b]Enabled[/b] — Master toggle (default: true)
[*][b]MuseumProgressBonusPercent[/b] — Extra chance for unowned trinkets per 10% aquarium completion (default: 5)
[*][b]MinimumMuseumProgress[/b] — No bonus below this donation fraction (default: 0.2 = 20%)
[*][b]MaxBonusChancePercent[/b] — Upper cap on the computed bonus (%), regardless of progress (default: 75)
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[size=4][b]Optional integrations[/b][/size]
[list]

[*][b]Haven Dev Tools[/b] — Trinket Fortune panel under Extensions (missing trinkets + probability when data is available). [i]Not required[/i] — the mod does not hard-depend on Dev Tools.
[/list]
