[b][size=5]Haven's Mirror[/size][/b]

Drop custom PNG or JPG bust portraits into a gallery folder and see your farmer in dialogue. Nested folders, loose names, and gallery-root fallback work. Optional forced look for NPC busts and seasonal outfits. The bust hides when the museum portrait closes.

[line]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Custom player dialogue bust from drop-in PNG or JPG gallery folders (nested subfolders and doubled extensions like [b]spring.png.png[/b] work)
[*]Per-character folders under [b]BepInEx/plugins/HavensMirror/gallery/[/b] (auto-created from save slots; names match loosely) plus [b]gallery/_shared/[/b], with images dropped in [b]gallery/[/b] as last fallback
[*]Seasonal / special slots: [b]spring.png[/b], [b]summer.png[/b], [b]autumn.png[/b], [b]winter.png[/b], [b]vows.png[/b], [b]shore.png[/b], [b]costume.png[/b] ([b].jpg[/b] also works)
[*]Player bust anchors on the left opposite the NPC, sized to match, and hides when that portrait closes (including the Museum donation screen)
[*]Optional [b]ForcedLook[/b] overrides NPC dialogue bust season / outfit
[*]Reload portraits in-game with [b]Ctrl+F8[/b] (either Ctrl key; configurable)
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx]BepInEx 5.x[/url] for Sun Haven
[*]Sun Haven
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Place [b]HavensMirror.dll[/b] in [b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/plugins/HavensMirror/[/b]
[*]Launch once so gallery folders and [b]HOWTO.txt[/b] appear
[*]Drop transparent PNG or JPG busts into [b]gallery/_shared/[/b], [b]gallery/YourCharacterName/[/b], or directly in [b]gallery/[/b]
[/list]

[line]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[b]Sun Haven/BepInEx/config/HavensMirror.cfg[/b]

[list]
[*][b]General.Enabled[/b] - Master toggle
[*][b]ForcedLook[/b] - Optional NPC bust look override
[*][b]Hotkeys[/b] - Portrait reload (default Ctrl+F8)
[/list]
